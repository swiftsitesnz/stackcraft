The process

Clear from the
first conversation.

A straightforward route from an operational problem to a working solution.

01

Understand

Start with the problem, not a feature list.

We talk through the work, the users and the constraints. You get a clear scope, proposed timeline and fixed-price quote before the build starts.

Scope · Timeline · Quote

02

Build

See it take shape.

Work happens in short cycles with previews and direct updates. If the scope changes, we agree the effect on cost and timing before the extra work begins.

Working previews · Direct feedback

03

Ship

Ready for the real world.

The build is checked across relevant screen sizes and user journeys, then deployed. Source code, setup notes and a walkthrough support the handover.

Deployment · Documentation · Handover

04

Support

Keep moving forward.

Agree the level of ongoing support you need, from ad-hoc changes to maintenance or further development. Support scope and charges are discussed separately.

Maintenance · Improvements · Next steps

Consultancy, coaching and team sessions are scoped around the engagement rather than a software delivery cycle.
