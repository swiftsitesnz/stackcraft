Privacy

Your enquiry.
Handled with care.

Information about contacting Stackcraft through this website.

What the form collects

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, the service you’re interested in and your message. These details are used to respond to your enquiry and discuss your project.

Where your message goes

Enquiries are emailed to Eli at Stackcraft using Resend, the email delivery service. The website and contact endpoint are hosted on Vercel. These providers process information needed to operate their services.

Keep sensitive information out of the brief

Please do not include passwords, access credentials, client records or sensitive personal information. If your project needs confidential material, discuss an appropriate way to share it with Eli first.

Questions or requests

For questions about your enquiry details, or to request access, correction or deletion, contact eli@stackcraft.co.nz.
