About

I design, build, and ship the software behind Stackcraft — and I run the consultancy, coaching, and training engagements the same way: directly, without layers between you and the person actually doing the work.

My background is operational, not academic. Before writing a line of code for a client, I've usually spent time understanding how the business actually runs — where the manual work piles up, where a "quick fix" would just move the problem somewhere else. That's the part most software people skip, and it's the part that decides whether what gets built actually gets used.

I use AI tools deliberately, throughout the process — not as a shortcut, but as a genuine multiplier on speed without lowering the bar. The same judgment, considerably faster delivery.

How I work

A short list of things I actually believe.

The process comes before the code

Automating a broken process just makes it fail faster. I look at how work actually happens before deciding what to build or change.

Regulated and compliance-sensitive work is a specialty, not a caveat

Healthcare and other regulated environments come with real constraints on what AI and automation can and can't do. I work inside those constraints properly, not around them.

Direct communication isn't a startup quirk

You talk to me, every time — not because it's scrappy, but because that's genuinely how senior work should be delivered.

Built to be handed off

Clean documentation and architecture your team can actually pick up, whether that's in a month or three years.
